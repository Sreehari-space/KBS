# Astra RevisionDesk: product decision
Research reviewed 10 September 2026. This is a product hypothesis, not proof of willingness to pay or guaranteed revenue.

## Recommended buyer and job
Freelance website builders and small web agencies with fixed-price projects, written deliverables and repeated client revisions. Capture a request from the browser, assign its agreed revision round, estimate extra work and issue a client-reviewable proposal before doing the work.

## Evidence and competing alternatives
- [BugHerd pricing](https://bugherd.com/pricing): Standard $50/month, or $42/month billed annually, for five members when reviewed. It offers a broader visual-feedback and project workflow. Paid category evidence is not proof that our narrower product will sell.
- [ScopePilot](https://getscopepilot.com/): $14.99/month advertised for scope documents, limits, change orders and client approvals. This directly overlaps our idea; its AI features were marked coming soon.
- [ScopePilot.io](https://scopepilot.io/): a separate competitor offering a free tier and Pro at €4.99/month when reviewed. We cannot claim to be the only affordable option.
- [SimpleCommenter free feedback tool](https://www.simplecommenter.com/tools/free-website-feedback-tool): another reason screenshots and annotations alone are a weak paid proposition.
- [Upwork on client expectations](https://www.upwork.com/resources/client-expectations): supports defining project scope and revision expectations clearly.
- [Markup alternatives discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/webdesign/comments/1ibl83q/markupio_alternatives/): a January 2025 poster said $25/month had been worthwhile but a $79 price was too high. This is one self-reported anecdote, not verified conversion or market-size data.
- [Freelancer scope-creep discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/freelance/comments/1ozc3zq/lost_2300_to_scope_creep_on_one_project_how_do/): illustrates extra work and awkward pricing conversations; self-reported losses are not independently verified.

Prices can change. Currencies, taxes, seats and feature scope differ; no like-for-like savings percentage is claimed.

## Shortlist decision
| Candidate | Reason to pay | Why not the flagship |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshot / visual feedback | Less client communication friction | Many free substitutes; weak switching incentive |
| Analytics tag QA | Agencies avoid reporting errors | [Omnibug](https://omnibug.io/about/) is free and open source; [Analytics Debugger](https://www.analytics-debugger.com/en/tools/analytics-debugger-extension/) already covers many vendors |
| Generic AI replies / summaries | Saves typing | Easy to reproduce in general AI tools; recurring inference costs |
| Revision-to-proposal workflow | Links extra work to a decision before delivery | Selected, but direct competitors and manual spreadsheets remain credible substitutes |

## Differentiation to test
Browser capture plus explicit evidence review, per-deliverable consolidated revision rounds, integer-money estimates, immutable issued copies and a recorded client response. No claim to automatic contract interpretation. A screenshot, project note or spreadsheet remains sufficient for many buyers. The product must be substantially quicker in recurring use to justify payment.

## Price hypothesis
Free: 1 active project, 10 requests/project, 3 issued proposals/month.
Pro: ₹2,499 for 365 days, manually renewed; 20 active projects, 100 requests/project, 50 issued proposals/month.
This is approximately ₹208/month when divided by twelve, charged annually once checkout is enabled. It is an unvalidated proposed price, not a proven optimum. Start with a paid pilot rather than lowering price until support becomes uneconomic.

The core does not require an AI API, so there is no per-request model bill. Hosting, object storage, transaction fees, taxes, support and acquisition still cost money. Do not promise lifetime hosted service or unlimited screenshots. Measure cost per active paying project before expanding limits. Revenue is not profit: 20 customers at ₹2,499 would be ₹49,980 gross annual billings, an arithmetic scenario rather than a forecast.

## Validation before commercial launch
Run ten qualified walkthroughs with freelancers who can show recent revision conversations; do not contact anyone without the owner's authorization. Ask them to complete a real proposal unaided. Aim for at least three paid pilot commitments at the proposed price, repeated use in a second project and a clear reduction in preparation time versus their existing process. These are decision thresholds, not research results.

Stop or change direction if users only want free annotation, rarely negotiate additional fees, avoid asking clients to open another link, or will not pay despite repeated use. Test a simple PDF/email handoff as an alternative. Distribution should focus on agency communities and concrete demonstrations of the workflow; no scraped outreach or fabricated testimonials.

## Implemented preview and launch boundaries
The package captures selected text, a stripped source URL and optionally the visible screenshot on explicit use. The hosted app stores project data, optional reviewed screenshots and issued proposals. Scope/rates lock before issuing. Review links expire after 30 days and support revocation; responses are self-reported, not verified identity or certified signatures. A proposal is not a payment receipt.

The current Site is private. Client links obey the Site audience and are not accessible to arbitrary external clients. Public availability or explicit client access must be arranged before client pilots. Razorpay merchant credentials, live payment tests, browser installation QA and Chrome Web Store publication remain launch work. No revenue or customer validation is claimed.

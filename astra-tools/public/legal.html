Privacy

In the original local tools, your selected images, receipts and screenshots are processed on your device. We do not upload these files to the Astra Tools service. ShareSafe captures only when you click its extension icon; it is not a background recorder.

Licences, free-use counts and DownloadRules settings are kept in your browser’s local storage or extension storage. Removing browser data can remove these settings. You can export rules and save a licence file.

When you buy, your email and selected product are sent to the purchase service and Razorpay. Razorpay handles payment information and stores the order and payment record. Astra Tools verifies captured payments to issue or recover a licence. We do not receive your full card details.

The hosting and payment providers may process connection and transaction data needed to deliver their services. This site has no advertising trackers. Contact support for questions about purchase records or data requests; records may need to be retained for applicable accounting obligations.

StepGuide stores your guide and screenshots locally; screenshots may include sensitive information visible in the recorded tab. ReplyReady stores templates and field values locally and reads shortcut text only on enabled websites. WatchDesk opens selected websites in background tabs to check their text; those websites receive normal browser requests, including existing sign-in cookies. FollowUp Desk stores conversation URLs, titles, notes and message identifiers locally. Its optional Gmail integration runs only on opened Gmail pages and does not send messages. Browser notifications may display monitor or conversation titles. Backups can contain private information; keep them secure.

RevisionDesk: project names, client names, scope, rates, request text and source paths are saved in hosted storage linked to your signed-in account. Screenshots are uploaded only when you select Include and save. Issued proposals keep a separate snapshot, including any attached screenshot. Removing an attachment from a request does not remove it from an already issued proposal. Archiving hides an active project but does not delete it. Ask support to delete hosted project records. Review links expire after 30 days and can be revoked while pending; issued records remain until deletion is arranged. Anyone with an active link and permitted Site access can read its contents. Response names are self-reported. Export a copy before requesting deletion.

Purchase terms

FileFit, ClaimPack, ShareSafe and DownloadRules are one-time purchases for the current major version. RevisionDesk, StepGuide, ReplyReady, WatchDesk and FollowUp Desk offer prepaid annual Pro licences: 365 days from the payment timestamp, with no automatic renewal. Renew by purchasing a new licence when the old term ends; early purchases do not stack unused time. Expiry restores free limits without deleting saved work. You may activate it in your own browser profiles; do not resell or publicly share your licence. Future major versions may be offered separately.

Listed INR prices are the total charged by this checkout. Check your bank’s terms for any independent charges. The free version lets you assess suitability before purchasing. Store approval and installation availability are stated separately from product functionality.

Keep original files and review generated results. FileFit cannot guarantee acceptance by an external application. ClaimPack does not verify expenses or provide accounting advice. ShareSafe requires you to select sensitive areas. DownloadRules affects only new browser downloads and cannot organise arbitrary existing folders.

WatchDesk is local monitoring, not a cloud service or guaranteed real-time alert system. FollowUp Desk flags observed thread activity for manual review, not verified replies. Both require Chrome running. StepGuide records supported browser controls, not desktop applications. ReplyReady does not promise shortcut support in all editors. Current limits are listed on each product page.

RevisionDesk classifications reflect the scope and estimates you enter, not a legal interpretation. Review scope, evidence, prices and taxes before sending. A recorded response is not verified identity, a certified signature or proof of payment. No recovery of fees or business income is guaranteed.

Refunds and purchase help

For duplicate payments, failed activation or a tool that does not work as described, contact support with the payment ID and a description of the issue. We will investigate and arrange a fix or an appropriate refund. Refunds, when issued, return through the original payment method; processing time depends on the provider. This does not limit any rights available under applicable law.

Developer previews

Preview packages are installed manually and may contain limitations described on the product page. Payment remains unavailable until the merchant has configured and enabled checkout. No browser-store approval or compatibility with every website is implied.

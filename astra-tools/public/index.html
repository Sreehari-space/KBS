GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

How do the prices work?

FileFit, ClaimPack, ShareSafe and DownloadRules are one-time purchases for version 1. RevisionDesk, StepGuide, ReplyReady, WatchDesk and FollowUp Desk use annual Pro licences lasting 365 days from payment. They do not renew automatically. After expiry, the free limits apply; saved work remains available. RevisionDesk projects are hosted; other tools store work locally.

Do my files leave my device?

Image processing, receipt assembly and screenshot editing happen in your browser or extension. Those files are not sent to our servers. RevisionDesk saves project details and screenshots you explicitly choose to upload. Checkout and licence activation use the purchase service and Razorpay.

Can I install the extensions today?

Developer preview ZIPs are available on each product page. Extract the ZIP, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Store installation links will appear after the listings are approved.

What works on mobile?

FileFit, ClaimPack and ShareSafe’s image editor can run on modern mobile browsers. Chrome extensions and DownloadRules require a supported desktop browser. Large files may need a desktop.

Where is my purchase?

Use My purchases to recover a licence with the payment ID and email used at checkout. Save your licence file after payment so you can activate it again.
